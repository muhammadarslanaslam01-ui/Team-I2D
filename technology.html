Engineering Design

From Cellulose to FDCA

A four-block integrated biorefinery pathway converting dissolving pulp to polymer-grade FDCA with full solvent and catalyst recovery.

Process Flow

The Four Transformation Blocks

Step-by-step chemical and enzymatic conversion pathway.

01

Enzymatic Hydrolysis Block 1

Dissolving pulp is slurried to 20 wt% cellulose and hydrolyzed to glucose over Cellic® CTec3 cellulase, reaching 90% conversion. Inert xylan and unreacted cellulose are filtered off, and ultrafiltration recovers 97% of the enzyme for reuse.

50°C
pH 4.8
120 h Residence
15 FPU/g Loading
02

Isomerization & SMB Separation Block 2

Glucose is isomerized over immobilized glucose isomerase on SiO₂, reaching a 42% conversion per pass. Simulated Moving Bed (SMB) chromatography on a Ca²⁺ cation-exchange resin isolates 96 wt% fructose, recycling the glucose-rich raffinate back to the reactor.

60°C
Glucose Isomerase on SiO₂
96% Fructose Purity
03

HMF Synthesis Block 3

Fructose is dehydrated to 5-hydroxymethylfurfural (HMF) in a 50:50 GVL/water medium, self-catalysed by recycled FDCA instead of a mineral acid. The liquid-full column reactor converts 95.6% of the feed at 70% single-pass yield to HMF before humins are removed over activated carbon.

180°C
FDCA-Catalysed (Acid-Free)
50:50 GVL/Water
70% HMF Yield
04

FDCA Oxidation Block 4

HMF is oxidized to FDCA over base-free 5 wt% Pt/C in a cooled multitubular packed bed at 91% single-pass yield. The liquor is hot-filtered, crystallized down to 8°C, washed, and dried. Solvent is recovered via distillation, and levulinic acid by-product is hydrogenated back to GVL.

110°C
40 bar O₂
Base-Free Pt/C
91% Single-Pass Yield
