Why We Need to Move Beyond PET

Global plastic production reached 400 million tonnes in 2022, with polyesters representing nearly one-quarter. PET's fossil-derived origin and end-of-life crisis demand a sustainable alternative.

400M Tonnes/year

Global plastic production continues to rise, with the vast majority derived from fossil fuels and lacking adequate recycling infrastructure.

PET's cradle-to-grave carbon footprint is substantial, synthesized exclusively from petroleum-derived TPA and MEG.

End-of-Life Crisis

Most end-of-life PET is landfilled, incinerated, or lost to the environment — the absence of recycling infrastructure is critical.
